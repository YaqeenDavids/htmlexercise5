Review: Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is one of my favourite technologies because it helps automate tasks, improve productivity, and solve complex problems. AI-powered tools are becoming increasingly useful in education, business, healthcare, and everyday life. Its ability to learn from data makes it one of the most exciting innovations of our time.

Review: Cloud Computing

Cloud Computing allows users to store data and access applications over the internet instead of relying solely on local devices. Services such as Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud provide flexibility, scalability, and reliability. I appreciate cloud technology because it makes collaboration easier and enables access to files from anywhere.
